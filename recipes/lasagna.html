Lasagna

These are the best lasagna you could ever taste !

Everyone who have tried this recipe ask me to do it all the time

Ingredients

  • Meat
  • Tomatoes
  • Pasta

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients
  2. Preheat the oven to 180°C
  3. Cook the meat with the tomatoes
  4. Put one layer of pasta then one layer of tomato meat
  5. Bake in the oven for 45 minutes
  6. Enjoy your Lasagna
